Healthy Veggie Bowl

Description

This healthy veggie bowl is a simple, colorful, and nutritious meal made with
fresh vegetables and perfectly cooked eggs. The combination of broccoli, sweet
corn, and juicy cherry tomatoes creates a delicious balance of flavors and textures.

It is quick and easy to prepare, making it a perfect choice for breakfast, lunch,
or a light dinner. Packed with protein, vitamins, and fresh ingredients, this dish
is both healthy and satisfying.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • Broccoli
  • Sweet corn
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Fresh parsley

Instructions

  1. Wash and prepare the vegetables.
  2. Boil or steam the broccoli.
  3. Fry the eggs in a pan.
  4. Arrange the vegetables in a bowl.
  5. Add the eggs on top.
  6. Season with salt and pepper.
  7. Serve and enjoy.
