Beef Tacos

Description

These beef tacos are packed with juicy, flavorful meat and wrapped in soft
tortillas with fresh toppings. The combination of grilled beef, onions, herbs,
and savory sauce creates a rich and delicious taste in every bite.

They are quick to prepare and perfect for lunch, dinner, or casual gatherings
with friends and family. Served warm and loaded with fresh ingredients, these
tacos are both satisfying and full of authentic flavor.

Ingredients

  • Soft tortillas
  • Beef strips
  • Onion
  • Fresh cilantro
  • Green onions
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Taco sauce or hot sauce

Instructions

  1. Season the beef with salt and black pepper.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and cook the beef until browned.
  3. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan.
  4. Slice and prepare the onions and herbs.
  5. Fill each tortilla with cooked beef.
  6. Add onions, cilantro, and sauce on top.
  7. Serve warm and enjoy.
