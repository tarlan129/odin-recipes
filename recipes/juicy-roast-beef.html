"Juicy Roast Beef"

Description

This juicy roast beef is tender, flavorful, and perfectly cooked with
a rich golden-brown crust. Seasoned with black pepper, salt, garlic,
and fresh rosemary, the meat stays soft and juicy on the inside while
developing a delicious smoky flavor on the outside.

It is an excellent dish for dinner, family gatherings, or special occasions.
Served hot and freshly sliced, this roast beef pairs perfectly with vegetables,
potatoes, or your favorite sauce.

Ingredients

  • Beef roast
  • Fresh rosemary
  • Garlic
  • Butter
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven and prepare the beef.
  2. Season the meat with salt, pepper, garlic, and rosemary.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and sear the beef on all sides.
  4. Transfer the beef to the oven and roast until cooked to your liking.
  5. Let the meat rest for a few minutes after cooking.
  6. Slice the roast beef and serve warm.
